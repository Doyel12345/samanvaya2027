About SAMANVAYA 2027

A two-day international conference at IIT Mandi that brings researchers in pure mathematics, applied mathematics and statistics into one room, on 31 May and 1 June 2027.

The conference exists to make pure and applied mathematics talk to each other

Mathematics has grown into many specialised communities that rarely share a seminar room. SAMANVAYA 2027 is a deliberate exception: a single, compact meeting where an algebraist, a numerical analyst and a statistician hear one another's work and find common questions.

The programme pairs keynote lectures that address a broad mathematical audience with contributed talks in two parallel tracks. Keynote speakers are asked to explain why their problems matter beyond their own area. Contributed sessions mix established researchers with early-career participants, so that discussion crosses both subject and seniority.

A particular aim is to give PhD students and postdoctoral researchers a visible place in the programme. Contributed talks and the poster session are open to them on equal terms, and the poster session is scheduled on the first evening so that conversations started there can continue through the second day.

The name

समन्वय (samanvaya) is a Sanskrit word, also in everyday use in Hindi, meaning synthesis, harmony or coordination: the bringing together of separate parts into a coherent whole.

It describes the conference's purpose directly: pure and applied mathematics treated as one discipline with shared methods, rather than as neighbours.

Two days, one plenary hall and two parallel tracks

Monday 31 May and Tuesday 1 June 2027. The detailed timetable appears on the Speakers & programme page.

8

Keynote lectures

Four on each day, delivered in plenary to all participants and chosen to span pure and applied areas.

20

Contributed talks

Ten per day in two parallel tracks, five talks per track per day, selected from submitted abstracts.

1

Poster session

On the evening of Day 1, with priority for work by PhD students and postdoctoral researchers.

1

Conference dinner

On the evening of Day 1, following the poster session, for all registered participants.

Track A

Pure Mathematics

Algebra and number theory, analysis, geometry and topology, discrete mathematics, and related areas.

Track B

Applied Mathematics & Statistics

Differential equations, numerical analysis, optimisation, probability and statistics, mathematical modelling and the mathematics of AI.

The conference is open to anyone who works with mathematics

Participants are expected from universities, research institutes and industry, in India and abroad. No particular area is required: the programme is designed so that a participant from any field can follow the keynote lectures.

Registration categories and fees are set out separately for students, academics and industry participants.

  • Faculty
    Members of mathematics, statistics and allied departments in universities and colleges.
  • Researchers
    Scientists at research institutes and laboratories whose work uses mathematical or statistical methods.
  • Postdocs
    Early-career researchers, encouraged to present talks and posters.
  • Students
    PhD and MSc students in mathematics, statistics and related subjects.
  • Industry
    Practitioners in data science, finance, engineering and computing.

IIT Mandi is a young institute in the Himalayan foothills

The Indian Institute of Technology Mandi was established in 2009 and is one of the newer IITs. Its main campus is at Kamand, in the valley of the Uhl river, about 15 km from Mandi town in Himachal Pradesh.

The campus lies in the foothills of the Himalaya, and participants should expect mountain weather and terrain. Practical information on reaching Kamand is on the Travel page.

The host school covers mathematics and statistics together

The School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences (SMSS) offers a BTech in Mathematics and Computing, an MSc programme, and a PhD programme.

Research in the school spans algebra, analysis, differential equations, numerical analysis, probability and statistics, optimisation and related areas, which is reflected in the two tracks of this conference.
