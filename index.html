Bringing the branches of mathematics into one room

SAMANVAYA, Sanskrit for synthesis or harmony, is a two-day conference hosted by the School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences at IIT Mandi.

It brings together researchers working across pure mathematics, applied mathematics and statistics, from algebra and topology to numerical methods, data science and mathematical modelling. Keynote lectures by leading mathematicians run alongside contributed talks in two parallel tracks and a poster session that gives PhD students and early-career researchers a place to present their work.

The conference takes place on the Kamand campus of IIT Mandi, in the Uhl valley of the Himalayan foothills.

A campus in the Himalayan foothills

IIT Mandi's Kamand campus sits beside the Uhl river, about 15 km from Mandi town. Days in late May are warm, in the low 30s °C, with milder evenings; it is a good time to visit Prashar Lake, Rewalsar and the Kullu valley before or after the conference.
