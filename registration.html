Registration and submission

How to submit an abstract for a contributed talk or poster, what registration costs, how to pay, and where to stay during the conference.

Submit an abstract first, then register once it is accepted

Speakers and poster presenters submit an abstract for review and register after they receive a decision. Participants who are not presenting may register directly at any time before registration closes.

Every presenting author must hold a paid registration by the early-bird deadline for the contribution to appear in the programme. All dates on this page are tentative and will be confirmed when the call opens.

How to submit an abstract

Abstracts are accepted for contributed talks in either track and for the poster session. Topics and formatting rules are on the Call for papers page.

  1. Prepare your abstract with the conference template

    Write a single-page abstract of no more than 300 words using the LaTeX or Word template, which will be available from the Call for papers page. State the title, all authors with affiliations, the presenting author, and whether you prefer a talk or a poster.

  2. Submit it through the online portal

    Upload the abstract as a PDF through the submission portal between 1 December 2026 and 15 February 2027. You will receive an acknowledgement by email. You may revise the submission until the deadline.

  3. Receive the decision on 15 March 2027

    Each abstract is reviewed by members of the scientific committee. The decision, and whether the contribution is scheduled as a talk or a poster, is sent to the corresponding author by email.

  4. Register and pay the fee

    The presenting author completes the registration form and pays the fee, preferably by the early-bird deadline of 10 April 2027. Contributions without a registered presenter by that date may be withdrawn from the programme.

One fee per participant, by category. The early-bird rate applies to payments received on or before 10 April 2027.

The fee includes

  • Conference kit with programme and book of abstracts
  • Admission to all keynote lectures, contributed sessions and the poster session
  • Lunch on both days, and morning and afternoon tea
  • The conference dinner on the evening of 31 May
  • Certificate of participation or presentation

The fee does not include

  • Accommodation, which is booked and paid for separately (see accommodation below)
  • Travel to and from Mandi

Students must upload a letter from their supervisor or head of department confirming their status. The accompanying-person fee covers meals, tea and the conference dinner, but not the kit, sessions or certificate.

How to pay

Fees will be payable online through the institute's payment gateway (for example, SBI Collect) or by direct bank transfer to the institute's account. Details will be published here when registration opens.

  1. Pay the fee for your category by online payment or bank transfer.
  2. Keep the transaction reference number (UTR) and a screenshot or PDF of the receipt.
  3. Fill in the registration form and upload the transaction reference and receipt.
  4. You will receive a confirmation of registration by email within a few working days.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellations must be sent by email to the organisers, quoting the transaction reference.

  • Cancellations received on or before 30 April 2027: 80% of the fee is refunded.
  • Cancellations received after 30 April 2027: no refund.
  • A registered participant may transfer the registration to another person in the same category at no charge, by informing the organisers before the conference.

Refunds are made to the original payment account after the conference. Bank charges, if any, are deducted.

Visa and invitation letters

Foreign participants who need a visa may request a formal invitation letter after completing their registration. Please write to the organisers with your full name as in your passport, nationality, passport number and the title of your accepted contribution.

India offers an e-Conference visa for attending international conferences. Apply well in advance, as processing and conference clearances can take several weeks.

Indian participants may request a letter of acceptance for institutional travel approval.

Frequently asked questions

For anything not covered here, please contact the organisers.

Can I attend without presenting?

Yes. Anyone may register as a participant without submitting an abstract, until regular registration closes on 10 May 2027, subject to the capacity of the venue.

Can I submit more than one abstract?

Each participant may be the presenting author of one contribution. You may be a co-author on other submissions presented by your collaborators.

Does each co-author need to register?

Only the presenting author must register. Co-authors who wish to attend register in the usual way.

Will on-campus accommodation be guaranteed?

No. Rooms in the guest house and hostels are limited and are allotted in order of request. Participants who do not receive a room on campus can stay in Mandi town.

Will I receive a receipt for the registration fee?

Yes. A receipt in the name of the participant (or the paying institution, if requested in the form) will be issued for reimbursement purposes.
