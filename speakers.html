Speakers and programme

Eight keynote lectures and twenty contributed talks across pure and applied mathematics and statistics, over three days at IIT Mandi, 31 May – 2 June 2027.

Keynote speakers

Eight invited lectures, three on each of the first two days and two on the final day, of 50 minutes each including questions. Speakers are drawn from across the scope of the conference.
