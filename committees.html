Organising committees

SAMANVAYA 2027 is organised by the School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences, IIT Mandi, with the guidance of an advisory committee and the review work of a scientific programme committee.

The conference is held under the patronage of the Institute

The patrons and the Conference Chair oversee the conference on behalf of IIT Mandi and the host school.
