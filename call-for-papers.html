Call for papers and posters

SAMANVAYA 2027 invites original research contributions across pure and applied mathematics and statistics, for presentation as contributed talks or posters at IIT Mandi.

An invitation to contribute

We welcome unpublished research in every area of mathematics and statistics, from the most abstract to the most applied.

The name Samanvaya means synthesis, and the conference aims to bring pure and applied mathematicians into the same rooms. Contributions that cross the boundaries between areas, or that carry mathematical ideas into the sciences, engineering, economics and data, are particularly welcome.

Researchers at every stage are encouraged to submit: faculty members, postdoctoral fellows, doctoral and master's students, and researchers in industry. Abstracts are reviewed by the Scientific Programme Committee and accepted contributions are presented in person.

Scope

Submissions are organised into ten tracks. The subtopics are examples only; interdisciplinary work and topics not listed are equally welcome.

Algebra & Number Theory

Commutative algebra, representation theory, group theory, algebraic and analytic number theory, arithmetic geometry, cryptography and coding theory.

Analysis & Operator Theory

Real and harmonic analysis, functional analysis, operator algebras, spectral theory, complex analysis, function spaces.

Geometry & Topology

Differential geometry, algebraic topology, low-dimensional topology, algebraic geometry, symplectic geometry, topological data analysis.

Differential Equations & Dynamical Systems

Ordinary and partial differential equations, ergodic theory, bifurcation and chaos, control theory, fractional and delay equations.

Numerical Analysis & Scientific Computing

Finite element and spectral methods, numerical linear algebra, approximation theory, uncertainty quantification, high-performance computing.

Probability, Statistics & Data Science

Stochastic processes, statistical inference, Bayesian methods, high-dimensional statistics, time series, causal inference.

Discrete Mathematics & Combinatorics

Graph theory, enumerative and algebraic combinatorics, extremal combinatorics, design theory, combinatorial algorithms.

Optimisation & Operations Research

Convex and non-smooth optimisation, integer programming, variational inequalities, game theory, stochastic optimisation, scheduling.

Mathematical Modelling

Mathematical biology and epidemiology, fluid and continuum mechanics, mathematical physics, quantitative finance, engineering applications.

Mathematics of AI & Machine Learning

Statistical learning theory, optimisation for deep learning, approximation by neural networks, kernel methods, geometric and topological methods in learning.

Types of contribution

Each participant may present one contribution, either as a talk or as a poster. Please indicate your preference when submitting.

Contributed talk

Oral presentation in one of two parallel tracks: Track A, Pure Mathematics, or Track B, Applied Mathematics & Statistics.

  • Length20 minutes: 15 minutes of presentation and 5 minutes for questions
  • AbstractUp to 300 words
  • SlotsAbout 20 talks in total, so selection is competitive

Poster

Presented at the poster session on the afternoon of Day 1. Doctoral students are especially encouraged to submit posters.

  • FormatA0 portrait, 841 × 1189 mm
  • AbstractUp to 300 words
  • RecognitionBest poster awards, presented at the valedictory session

Publication of selected full papers in a proceedings volume or a journal special issue is being explored; details will be announced.

Submission guidelines

Abstracts are prepared in LaTeX using the conference template and submitted as a PDF through the submission portal. Each abstract should include:

  • the title of the contribution;
  • the names, affiliations and email addresses of all authors, with the presenting author indicated;
  • an abstract of up to 300 words;
  • 3–5 keywords;
  • one or more Mathematics Subject Classification (MSC 2020) codes;
  • the preferred mode of presentation (talk or poster) and the preferred track.

The presenting author must register for the conference. Each registered participant may give one presentation, but may be a co-author on further submissions presented by others.

Review process

All abstracts are reviewed by the Scientific Programme Committee for relevance to the scope of the conference, originality and clarity of presentation.

Because contributed talk slots are limited, some abstracts submitted for a talk may instead be offered a poster presentation.

Decisions will be communicated to the presenting author by email by 15 March 2027.

Frequently asked questions

For anything not covered here, please write to samanvaya2027@iitmandi.ac.in or see the contact page.

Can I present both a talk and a poster?

No. Each registered participant may give one presentation. Abstracts submitted for a talk that are not selected for the oral programme may be offered a poster instead.

Is there an online option for presenting?

The conference is planned as an in-person event, and all presentations are expected to be given on site. This is to be confirmed and any change will be announced on this website.

In which language should abstracts and presentations be given?

All abstracts, talks and posters should be in English.

Will certificates be issued?

Yes. Certificates of participation and of presentation will be issued to registered participants.

Is travel support available for students?

Limited travel support for students may be available. Details and the application procedure will be announced.

Can undergraduate students submit posters?

Yes. Undergraduate students are welcome to submit posters on their research or project work, ideally with the support of a faculty supervisor.
